NexusOS 1.4.3

- Windows stack properly: every NexusOS app is now a real window, so clicking Files or Settings brings them in front of Firefox, Steam or anything else. The taskbar stays on top, and Start and quick settings open over everything.
- Taskbar: right-click any app to pin or unpin it (Steam, Discord, Firefox too). Pinned apps are there every time you start. Right-click also lists an app's windows and can close them. The thin strip at the far right shows the desktop.
- Right-click menus everywhere: new folder, new text document, cut, copy, paste, rename, delete, properties, and "Set as desktop background" on pictures.
- The Bin, top-left on the desktop: drag things onto it, open it to restore or delete for good, or empty it.
- Desktop: drag icons wherever you like (they stay put), select several with a box, drop files onto folders or the Bin, and drag files from Files onto the desktop.
- Picture backgrounds: right-click a picture, or pick one in Settings > Appearance.
- Rename files, folders and USB drives (right-click, or F2).
- Date & time: turn "Set time automatically" on or off, and set the date and time yourself.
- Task Manager (Ctrl+Shift+Esc): CPU, memory, NVIDIA GPU, disk and network, with End task.
- Window frames in NexusOS colours. Alt + right-drag resizes any window.
